Wipe the palm over the to the porta filter basket and trail fingers over the lugs to remove any loose coffee grounds GRINDER CLEANING PROCEDURE Switch off the machine. Remove the bean hopper and keep the remaining coffee beans in a dark airtight container Put back the empty grinder hopper, and then switch on the machine. Remove the remaining coffee beans by pressing with the standard base porta filter until machine stops dispensing the coffee grounds. Transfer grinder cleaner tablets into the cap of container (approximately 30 grams) and put it in the bean hopper. Draw the grinder cleaner by pressing standard base mode until its completely dispensed   Transfer back the coffee beans you removed earlier and dispense two standard bases. Make sure that there is no remaining residue of cleaning powder before serving an order. * Grinder cleaning should be done every 1st of the month before opening shift starts. STEAM WAND CLEANING PROCEDURE Remove the cap and press the bottle until you fill up the detergent up to 20 ml. Prepare the Milk Cleaner Solution (20 ml of Milk cleaner into 500 ml of warm water). Soak the steam wand for 5 minutes into the solution then rinse with clean warm water. (Use the jug that has milk stone build up,  milk cleaner also removes the stain in the jug.). Wipe the steam wand with sanitized towel. Activate the wand 3 to 5 seconds.  *Steam wand cleaning should be done on daily basis. | RANCILLO COFFEE MACHINE DAILY CLEANING   Clean housing with damp cloth.
