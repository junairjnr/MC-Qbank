7. Before making coffee, place the portafilter
into the group heads to warm up. Leave the
handles unlocked and resting in the group
heads. Locking them will put unnecessary stress
on the group head seals.
2. Power on the main switch 30 minutes prior to
opening. Wait for 5 minutes to fill up the boiler.
3. After 5 minutes, switch on the boiler heating
element. Machine will heat for approximately
30 minutes. After 10 minutes the machine must
be purged of any airlocks by momentarily
opening both steam valves in turn. The
machine is ready for use when the pressure
gauge indicates pressure between 0.95 to 1.05
bar.
8. Perform crema test by following the
procedures for making espresso. If crema is
not achieved, inform the MIC and call for
service.
9. Place the clean and sanitized cups onto the
warmer.  Allow them to heat before making 
coffee.
Remember:  
Make sure that the cups are free of water 
before placing on top of the warmer. Place 
portafilter basket side down on the drip tray.
4. Switch on the cup warmer only when the
cups are not warming enough.
5. Without portafilter handles in place, run all
group heads 3 to 4 times using small pour
button to circulate hot water through the
group heads.
Ensure to check the following prior to
operation
Water pressure - 8 to 9 bar
Water level
Coffee bar

morning. Fill the jug with hot water from the
outlet in the machine. Use the thermometer, 
temperature should be between 88C to 92C.

Daily cleaning procedure
Note!  Do not use abrasive sponges and detergents or solvents! If cleaner is required, follow the instruction 
in the package! Do not immerse machine into water!
1.  Clean housing with damp cloth.  Follow the 
grain of the satin finish on stainless steel parts.
2. Clean steam wand/ hot water spout.
3. Remove filter holders/ unlock the filters, brush/
clean all parts.
4.  Brush the outlets and the seals under the group 
head.
5. Rinse with hot water to remove all the coffee
bean residue.
6. Submerge steam wand and head with cleaning 
supply.
Cleaning of group head
The machine has to be under pressure.
1. Hold the corresponding button of the left 
group until   Coffee   Cleaning   is   displayed(5 
seconds).
2.Follow the on-screen information as follows: 
Coffee Cleaning? Press [Enter]
Insert blind filter and add the detergent (1/4 
espresso spoon for each group head. 
A Cleaner [Enter] 
The groups are cleaned alternately.
3.Remove blindfilter disk. Lock the 
filter holder  again then [Enter].
4.Press button D after cleaning
Remove the filter holder, knock it out and lock it 
again.
Do not damage the filters.
5. Switch off cup warmer, boiler heating 
element and the main switch.
Do not serve the 1st coffee produced in the morning to remove the taste after cleaning.

the coffee grounds.

until it’s  completely dispensed.
GRINDER CLEANING PROCEDURE

dispense two standard bases. Make sure that there is no 
remaining residue of cleaning powder before serving an order.
