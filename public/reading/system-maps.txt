System Maps - Objectives
To ensure that our restaurant teams have the tools to run great restaurants, the 
9 Restaurant System Maps help improve operational performance, accurately 
diagnose opportunities and realize potential. The maps help optimize restaurant 
systems to consistently execute as designed to deliver a great customer 
experience, promote McDonald’s Values and People brand standards, business 
growth, and return on investment.
The maps can also be used as a stand-alone resource to develop newly 
promoted leaders.
