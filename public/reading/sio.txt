Pre-Shift (estimated time 1 to 1.5 hours)
Read all communications, discuss with previous with the Shift Leader, including relevant information from the day that will impact 
the shift, including any opportunities, trends (e.g., Sales/GCs or product mix trends), problems, or barriers for which you will need 
to take action.
