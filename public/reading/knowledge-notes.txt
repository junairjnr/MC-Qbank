Dual Point Service (DPS)
A walk up service process where a customer go to counter to order ( so they don’t line up behind people waiting for food) with an Order Ready Board (ORB ) that tells the customer their order is ready and comes to collect their completed order in the pick-up area.
Receipt to Present (R2P)-90” seconds 
Time starts when customer receipt prints until when the full order is presented to the customer.
Line Time (90 seconds)
Time starts when the customers get in line and stops when the customer is able to order
OUT OF ORDER SEQUNCE PRESENTING (OOSP)
One of the benefits of DPS which reduces the Total Experience time of the customer(small simple orders can be served before the large orders are ready)
